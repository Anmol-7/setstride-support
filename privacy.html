SetStride Privacy Policy

This page includes information about SetStride 2.0 features. Subscription plans, portable backup files, training reminders and workout Live Activity controls described below apply to version 2.0 when released. Existing version 1.0.0 uses the earlier trial and Full Unlock purchase model; its original purchase terms remain available here. The original version 1.0.0 privacy policy, effective September 29, 2026, is preserved here.

Your workout data

SetStride is a local-first workout logger. Workouts, sets, exercise notes, routines, custom exercises and preferences are stored in the app's database on your device. You do not need a SetStride account. We do not operate a SetStride server that receives or synchronizes your workout database.

The app uses your records on-device to display workout history, calculate training statistics, resume an unfinished workout and suggest values from previous sessions. Estimates such as estimated one-rep maximum are calculations from recorded data, not medical assessments.

Files you import or share

SetStride can read workout CSV files you choose to import and create CSV exports you choose to share. In version 2.0, you can also create and select portable `.setstride.json` backup files. These backups can contain your saved workouts and notes, routines, exercise data, preferences, training plans and an unfinished workout when included. The app prepares backup files locally and can retain local safety copies when replacing data during a restore.

When you choose to share or save a file, the destination you select—such as Files, a storage provider or another app—receives that file. Its handling and retention are governed by that destination's practices. Sharing a file does not automatically turn on SetStride cloud synchronization. A prepared file still inside SetStride is not proof that a separate copy has been saved outside the app; verify the selected destination before deleting the app or moving devices. Backup files contain personal workout information, so choose their destination accordingly.

Purchases

Apple handles in-app purchases through your Apple Account. SetStride uses StoreKit purchase information supplied and verified by Apple to determine access, including product and transaction identifiers, original purchase information, purchase dates, and, where applicable, subscription expiration, upgrade and revocation information. SetStride uses purchase updates and restored purchases to reconcile access. Apple handles payment details and subscription billing under its own privacy practices.

In SetStride 2.0, monthly and annual Pro subscriptions are checked using verified purchase information. Existing Full Unlock purchases and original 30-day trial acquisitions are also recognized; restoring a legacy trial does not restart its original period. Purchase access information is separate from your workout backup: restoring purchases does not restore deleted workout data.

SetStride does not receive your payment-card details or require a separate app login to restore a purchase. Apple maintains purchase history associated with your Apple Account. The legacy non-consumable trial’s 30-day expiry is calculated from its verified original acquisition time, rather than an Apple subscription-expiry date.

Notifications and links

SetStride uses local notifications for rest-finished alerts when enabled and permitted by your device. In version 2.0, you can also configure local training reminders. Notification delivery and visibility depend on your system permissions and settings.

On supported devices, version 2.0 Pro can display an active workout through Apple's Live Activities on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. The app provides workout state to these system surfaces, such as the exercise name, set number, entered weight and reps, next-set context and rest-timer status. These details may be visible on your device's system surfaces according to its settings. Live Activity controls use the app's on-device workout state to apply supported actions; they do not create a separate cloud workout account. You can manage notification and Live Activity visibility through your device settings.

Opening a support, privacy or other external link contacts the destination website, which may process standard connection information according to its policy.

Advertising and diagnostics

SetStride does not include advertising tracking, a developer usage-analytics service, or a developer-hosted workout-data service. The app's Analytics tab calculates your training statistics locally; it is not a service that sends your usage or workouts to us. Apple's App Store, StoreKit, operating-system backup and optional diagnostics services operate under Apple's policies and your device/account settings. This policy does not promise that operating-system services never use a network connection.

Retention and deletion

Workout records remain in the local database until you delete them or remove the app's data. SetStride provides history deletion and a confirmed database reset. Uninstalling the app can remove its local records. Device backup or migration may retain copies depending on your Apple settings; this is not a SetStride cloud-sync guarantee.

Deleting local app data does not erase Apple's purchase history or restart a previously acquired trial. Files you exported, backups and copies held by other apps are not automatically deleted by a SetStride reset.

Contacting support

You decide what information to send to the support address above. Never send Apple Account passwords, verification codes or payment-card details. Send only the minimum information needed to explain your issue. If workout evidence is necessary, share a limited, redacted example rather than a complete workout export whenever possible. Screenshots can contain personal training information; redact anything you do not want to share.

The publisher monitors setstride.support@gmail.com, hosted by Google's Gmail service. We use your email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide to respond to your request and investigate the reported issue. We do not use support messages for advertising.

We delete resolved support messages and their attachments from the support mailbox 12 months after the recorded case-resolution date. We do not keep separately downloaded attachments after deleting their related case. You may request earlier deletion by emailing the same address. We may retain information needed for an unresolved issue or a legal obligation for as long as that need applies. This policy covers our support mailbox, not Apple's purchase records or copies held by you or other recipients.

Google processes email under its own Privacy Policy. Deletion from our mailbox does not promise immediate erasure from Google's backup or security systems. We do not promise that email processing is confined to your country.

Public website

The support and legal pages contain no advertising trackers, analytics scripts, contact forms or remotely loaded fonts added by SetStride. When these pages are served through GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. Its processing is governed by the GitHub Privacy Statement. Our 12-month support-mail retention policy does not set GitHub's infrastructure-log retention period.

Changes and questions

We will update this page when the app's data practices change and show its effective date. Contact the publisher above with privacy questions or requests concerning information you sent to support. Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, restrict or object to its use, obtain a portable copy, or complain to your local data-protection authority. We may ask for enough information to verify a request. We cannot retrieve a workout database stored only on your device; use the app's local export and deletion controls for that data.
