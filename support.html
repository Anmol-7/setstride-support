SetStride Support

Effective November 3, 2026

Choose your app version

The instructions below describe SetStride 2.0 when released. Check your app version in Settings. If you are using version 1.0.0, read the separate version 1.0.0 purchase guidance below; its earlier access rules are different.

Getting started in SetStride 2.0

Start an empty workout or choose a saved routine. Add an exercise, enter each set's values and tap Log. The app preserves your active workout so you can return to it. Finish the workout to save it in History and include it in relevant Analytics. Ordinary workout logging, finishing, history, editing, basic progress, unlimited custom exercises, starter sessions and up to four routines are Free and work offline. No SetStride account is needed.

Import and export workout history

In Settings, use Go to CSV Importer to import supported Strong or Hevy workout-history CSV files. Check exercise mappings and the import preview before confirming. Duplicate or overlapping records may be rejected to protect your data. Use Export as CSV to share saved workout history. CSV files contain personal workout information; choose their destination carefully. A history CSV is not a full backup of routines, settings or unfinished workouts.

Create a complete manual backup

Open Settings > Backup & restore > Create backup, then choose Share backup and save the file outside SetStride. Check that the file exists in your chosen destination before deleting the app or changing devices. A prepared file still inside the app is not proof that you have a separate copy. Full manual backup is Free; it does not turn on cloud synchronization.

To restore, open Settings > Backup & restore > Choose backup file. Review the backup before replacing your current data. Restoring replaces local training data rather than merging it. If a workout is active, finish or deliberately discard it before restoring; you can still create a backup while it is active. The Create safety copy & restore action prepares a local safety copy before replacement. Save any safety copy you may need outside the app using its sharing control.

SetStride 2.0 Free and Pro

SetStride Pro adds interactive workout Live Activities, planning and progression tools, plan comparisons, unlimited routine organisation, muscle-coverage suggestions, session preparation and adaptation, equipment profiles, training rotations and weekly reviews. Rest timers and rest-finished notifications remain Free. Lock Screen logging requires iOS 17 or later, a supported iPhone and Live Activities enabled for SetStride; Dynamic Island and Always-On presentation depend on hardware.

Open Settings > Access & restore for available monthly and yearly plans, localized prices and purchase terms. Both plans include the same Pro features and renew automatically unless canceled. Eligible Apple Accounts may receive a 14-day introductory free subscription period; unless canceled, it becomes the selected paid subscription at the terms shown by Apple. Opening the app does not start a trial or subscription. Use Manage subscription when available, or Apple's subscription settings, to manage or cancel a subscription. Deleting SetStride does not cancel it.

Existing verified Full Unlock purchases keep Pro access without another subscription. Existing 30-day trials retain their original expiry, do not restart on Restore and never convert into a subscription. When Pro ends in version 2.0, workouts, plans and routines remain saved. Ordinary Free logging, editing, recovery and finishing a workout remain available. See purchase information for complete version-specific rules.

Restore purchase access

Open Settings > Access & restore > Restore purchases to reconcile purchases associated with your Apple Account. Internet access or Apple sign-in may be required. Pending approval is not completed ownership. Never share your Apple password or verification code with support.

Restoring purchases does not restore workout data. Use a full manual backup to recover workout data. Purchase access and workout backups are separate.

Version 1.0.0: earlier trial and Full Unlock model

These purchase rules apply only to the installed SetStride 1.0.0 app, not to version 2.0: the 30-day trial requires a separate zero-price Apple confirmation. No automatic charge follows it. Full Unlock is a separate one-time purchase. Open Purchases & restore in Settings for its local offer and restoration controls. When that version's trial expires, Full Unlock is required to start, continue or finish a workout, including one already in progress. Your unfinished draft and logged sets remain on your device; they are not automatically completed or deleted. You can still read saved workout history, export saved workout history, delete local data and restore eligible purchases. See the original version 1.0.0 purchase terms.

Missing workout records

Restoring a purchase does not restore your workout database. Check whether you are opening the same app installation and device that held the records. Avoid uninstalling or resetting the app while troubleshooting missing data. Contact support before taking actions that could erase remaining local records. In version 2.0, preserve a full manual backup outside the app before replacing or deleting local data.

Report an issue

Send the app version, iPhone model, iOS version, steps leading to the issue, and what you expected instead. Attach a redacted screenshot if helpful. Do not include payment-card details, credentials, verification codes or sensitive notes. Only send workout exports if needed and you are comfortable sharing them.

The publisher monitors the support mailbox, but no fixed response time is guaranteed. Resolved messages and attachments are deleted 12 months after the recorded case-resolution date; you may request earlier deletion, except where information is needed for an unresolved issue or legal obligation. See the Privacy Policy for details.
