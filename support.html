SetStride Support

Getting started

Start an empty workout or choose a saved routine. Add an exercise, enter each set's values and tap Log. The app preserves your active workout so you can return to it. Finish the workout to save it in History and include it in relevant Analytics.

Import and export

Open Settings to import supported workout-history CSV files or export your existing history. Check exercise mappings and the import preview before confirming. Duplicate or overlapping records may be rejected to protect your data.

An exported history file contains personal workout information. Choose its destination carefully. It is not a full backup of routines, settings or unfinished workouts.

Trial and purchases

The 30-day trial requires a separate zero-price Apple confirmation. No automatic charge follows it. Full Unlock is a separate one-time purchase. Open Purchases & restore in Settings for the current local offer and restoration controls.

When the trial expires, Full Unlock is required to start, continue or finish a workout, including one already in progress. Your unfinished draft and logged sets remain on your device; they are not automatically completed or deleted. You can still read saved workout history, export saved workout history, delete local data and restore eligible purchases. See Purchase information for the complete access rules.

If access is not recognized, confirm you are using the Apple Account associated with the acquisition and try Restore Purchases with a network connection. Pending approval is not completed ownership. Never share your Apple password or verification code with support.

Missing workout records

Restoring a purchase does not restore your workout database. Check whether you are opening the same app installation and device that held the records. Avoid uninstalling or resetting the app while troubleshooting missing data. Contact support before taking actions that could erase remaining local records.

Report an issue

Send the app version, iPhone model, iOS version, steps leading to the issue, and what you expected instead. Attach a redacted screenshot if helpful. Do not include payment-card details, credentials, verification codes or sensitive notes. Only send workout exports if needed and you are comfortable sharing them.

The publisher monitors the support mailbox, but no fixed response time is guaranteed. Resolved messages and attachments are deleted 12 months after the recorded case-resolution date; you may request earlier deletion, except where information is needed for an unresolved issue or legal obligation. See the Privacy Policy for details.
