What SetStride provides

SetStride helps you record workouts, reuse routines, import supported workout files and inspect your recorded training progress. It is a tracking tool, not a personal trainer, medical service or guarantee of fitness results. Choose exercises and loads appropriate for you; app estimates and suggestions do not establish that an activity is safe for a particular person.

Optional 30-day trial

The trial begins only after you explicitly acquire the zero-price 30-day Trial through Apple's purchase confirmation. Installing or opening the app does not start it. The trial lasts 30 days from the verified original acquisition time. It does not automatically charge you or turn into a subscription.

Restoring an acquisition, reinstalling the app or deleting its local data does not create a new trial. Trial availability is checked against Apple's verified purchase history.

Full Unlock

Full Unlock is a separate one-time in-app purchase, not a recurring subscription. The Apple purchase screen displays the applicable local price and conditions before you confirm. Do not rely on a price quoted for a different country or an earlier app version.

Full Unlock covers this app's workout logging, imports, Analytics, routine creation/editing and saved-workout editing. It does not promise coaching, cloud storage, services or future separately sold products that the app does not offer. Eligible verified ownership remains subject to Apple's purchase and refund handling.

After the trial ends

Without verified Full Unlock access, you may continue to read existing workout history, export saved workout history as CSV, delete local data, and use Restore Purchases. CSV export is not a full backup of routines, preferences or unfinished workouts. Starting a new workout, importing workouts, using Analytics, creating/editing routines and editing saved workouts require access.

At trial expiry, Full Unlock is required to continue or finish any workout, including one already in progress. Its unfinished draft and logged sets remain saved on the device. The app does not silently complete or delete it. Restarting, pausing or restoring the workout does not restart the trial. Verified Full Unlock lets you resume the saved draft.

Restoring access and verification

Use Settings → Purchases & restore to restore eligible purchases associated with your Apple Account. Verification or restoration may require an internet connection or Apple sign-in. Closing a purchase sheet, a pending approval or an unverified transaction does not itself grant access.

Purchase restoration does not restore deleted workouts. SetStride stores workout data locally; protect important records with suitable exports and device backups. A workout-history CSV is not a complete app backup.
