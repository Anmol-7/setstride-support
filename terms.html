SetStride 2.0: Free features

Core in-app workout logging, saved workout history, workout editing, import and export, basic progress, recovery, starter sessions, unlimited custom exercises, up to four routines, manual backup and restore, in-app rest timers and rest-finished notifications are Free. These core workout features work offline. No SetStride account registration is required.

SetStride Pro

Pro adds workout Live Activity controls on supported Lock Screen and Dynamic Island devices, planning and progression tools, plan comparisons, unlimited routine organisation, muscle-coverage suggestions, session generation and adaptation, equipment profiles, training rotations and weekly training reviews. Availability of system surfaces depends on your device, operating system and permissions.

Monthly and annual subscriptions provide the same Pro features. The monthly plan bills for one month; the annual plan bills for one year. The localized price and billing period are shown in SetStride and Apple's purchase confirmation before you subscribe. Eligible customers may receive a 14-day free introductory subscription period. Apple determines eligibility; each customer can redeem one introductory offer in the subscription group. The available offer and the price billed afterward are shown before confirmation. Opening SetStride does not start a paid subscription or a new trial.

Billing, renewal and cancellation

Apple handles subscription purchases and billing through your Apple Account. For an eligible free introductory period, no subscription payment is charged during that period. Unless cancelled, the selected subscription then renews at the price shown in Apple's confirmation. Without a free introductory period, payment is charged when you confirm the purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through Apple. Review Apple's confirmation for your price, renewal date and applicable billing terms.

To view, change or cancel a subscription, open Settings > Access & restore > Manage subscription in SetStride, or use Apple's subscription settings. Cancel before renewal to prevent future renewal charges. Deleting SetStride does not cancel a subscription. Apple handles refund requests under its applicable policies; contact Apple Support for cancellation guidance.

When verified Pro access ends, existing workouts, plans and routines remain saved. Core Free logging, editing, recovery and finishing an ongoing workout remain available. Pro-only tools require active Pro access. Existing routines are retained if your total exceeds the Free limit; creating additional routines requires Pro while you are at that limit.

Existing Full Unlock and 30-day trial purchases

Existing verified Full Unlock purchases retain access to Pro features without a subscription. Full Unlock owners do not need to subscribe again.

An existing verified 30-day trial keeps its original expiry, based on the original acquisition time. Reinstalling or restoring does not restart it. It does not automatically become a subscription or charge you for one. In version 2.0, core Free features remain available after that legacy trial ends; Pro features require a valid existing Full Unlock or active subscription.

Restore purchases and protect your workout data

Open Settings > Access & restore > Restore purchases to ask Apple to reconcile purchases associated with your Apple Account. Internet access or Apple sign-in may be required to purchase or restore. SetStride relies on verified Apple purchase information to grant access.

Restoring purchases restores purchase access, not deleted workout data. Use SetStride's manual backup/export features to save your workout data and verify your chosen file destination before deleting the app or changing devices.

What SetStride provides

SetStride helps you record workouts, reuse routines, import supported workout files and inspect your recorded training progress. It is a tracking tool, not a personal trainer, medical service or guarantee of fitness results. Choose exercises and loads appropriate for you; app estimates and suggestions do not establish that an activity is safe for a particular person.
